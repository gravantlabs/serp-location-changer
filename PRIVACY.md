# Privacy policy

SERP Location Changer & Compare is a browser extension made by GRAVANT LABS LLC ("we"). This policy says what data the extension uses, where that data goes, and what you can do about it.

Effective date: 1 November 2026.

## In short

- We do not collect your data. We have no server that receives data from the extension.
- The extension sends data to two services only: Photon, to find places, and Google, when you search.
- Your places and settings stay in your browser.

## What the extension sends, and to whom

| Data | Sent to | When | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The text you type in the place search box | Photon (`photon.komoot.io`), run by komoot | When you search for a place | To find matching places |
| The latitude and longitude of the place you chose, and your Google language setting | Google (`google.com`) | When you search on Google while a place is on | So Google shows results for that place |

The extension adds the place to your Google searches. It does not make searches on its own.

Photon and Google handle this data under their own privacy policies. We do not control what they do with it.

## What stays in your browser

The extension stores these items in your browser's extension storage:

- the places you starred;
- the place that is on, and your language setting;
- the places in your last comparison;
- recent place search results, until you close the browser.

This data does not leave your browser. We cannot see it.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not read your browsing history.
- It does not read Google pages, except one line: the location that Google shows at the bottom of the results page. The extension uses it to show you a ✓ or ✗. That line stays in your browser.
- It does not use analytics, ads or trackers.
- It does not sell or share data.

## Chrome Web Store user data policy

The extension's use of information follows the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the Limited Use requirements.

## How to delete your data

- To delete a starred place, select its star again.
- To delete everything, remove the extension from your browser. Your browser then deletes all data the extension stored.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
